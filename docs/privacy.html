Scope

This policy describes how the Sinope Android application handles information. Sinope is a two-factor authentication app: it stores the shared secrets issued by your online services and uses them to generate time-based one-time passcodes (TOTP).

Sinope is operated entirely on your device. There is no Sinope account, no sign-in, and no backend service.

What is stored on your device

Sinope writes two kinds of information to its private storage area, which other applications cannot read.

Stored data
DataPurposeProtection
Accounts The issuer, account name, icon and colour you choose, and the shared secret used to generate codes. Each secret is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is generated inside the Android Keystore, which is designed so that key material cannot be extracted from the device.
Settings Your chosen language, whether the biometric lock is enabled, whether screenshot protection is enabled, and whether you have completed the introduction. Held in the application's private storage.

That is the complete list. Sinope does not store your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.

Network access

Sinope does not request the INTERNET permission. Android therefore prevents the application from opening any network connection whatsoever. This is not a policy commitment that could be quietly reversed — it is enforced by the operating system, and you can verify it yourself in the app's manifest.

The application contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking libraries.

Camera

Camera access is used for one purpose: reading the QR code your provider displays when you enable two-factor authentication. The camera is active only while the scanning screen is open.

Frames are decoded on your device by a barcode library bundled inside the application. No photograph or video is written to storage, and nothing is transmitted. Camera access is optional — every account can be added by entering its details manually instead.

Biometrics

If you enable the optional app lock, authentication is carried out entirely by Android's own biometric subsystem. Sinope receives a success or failure result and nothing more. It never receives, processes or stores your fingerprint, facial geometry or any other biometric data.

Backups you create

Sinope can export your accounts to a .sinope file so that you can move them to another device or retain a copy. When you do:

  • You choose the password. It is converted into an encryption key using Argon2id, and the contents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • You choose the destination, using Android's own file picker.
  • The password is never stored and cannot be recovered. If it is lost, the backup cannot be opened by anyone, including us.
  • The file belongs to you. Sinope does not upload it and has no capability to do so.

Once the file leaves the application, responsibility for it passes to you. If you place it in a cloud storage service, that provider's privacy policy governs the copy held there.

Android system backup

Sinope's account database is explicitly excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Your secrets are not copied off the device by the operating system.

Sharing and disclosure

Sinope does not share, sell, rent or disclose your information to anyone. No third party is involved, because no information ever leaves your device. There is consequently nothing that could be produced in response to a request from any party.

Children

Sinope is a general-purpose security utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from any user, of any age.

Your control and deletion

You may delete any individual account from within the app, or remove every account at once from Settings. Uninstalling Sinope erases its entire storage area, including all stored secrets. No copy remains elsewhere, because no copy was ever sent elsewhere.
