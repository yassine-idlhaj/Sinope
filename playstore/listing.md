## Full description  (limit 4000)

```
Sinope generates the two-factor authentication codes that protect your
accounts — entirely on your phone.

There is no Sinope account to create, no server to trust, and no way for the
app to send your secrets anywhere. Sinope does not request the INTERNET
permission, so Android itself prevents it from making any network connection.
That is not a promise in a privacy policy. It is enforced by the operating
system, and you can verify it yourself: the source code is public.


WHAT IT DOES

• Generates standard time-based codes (TOTP, RFC 6238) for any service that
  supports two-factor authentication — GitHub, Google, Microsoft, Dropbox,
  your bank, and thousands more
• Add accounts by scanning a QR code, or type the details in by hand
• Supports 6 or 8 digit codes, 30 or 60 second periods, and SHA-1, SHA-256
  and SHA-512
• Search your vault, mark favourites, and give each account its own icon
  and colour


BUILT TO KEEP SECRETS

• Every secret is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is generated inside the
  Android Keystore and is designed so it cannot be extracted from the device
• Your accounts are excluded from Android's cloud backup and from
  device-to-device transfer, so they are never copied off your phone by the
  system
• Optional biometric lock, so codes are only shown after you authenticate
• Optional screenshot protection, which stops the screen being captured or
  recorded while Sinope is open


BACKUPS YOU CONTROL

Export your accounts to an encrypted .sinope file whenever you want. The file
is protected with a password you choose, using Argon2id to derive the key and
AES-256-GCM to seal the contents.

The password is never stored and cannot be recovered — not by you, and not by
us. Keep it somewhere safe. Import the file on a new device to move everything
across in one step, with duplicates detected automatically.


NO ADS, NO TRACKING, NO ACCOUNTS

Sinope contains no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It
collects nothing, because it cannot send anything. There is no sign-up, no
email address, and no profile.


OPEN SOURCE

Sinope is released under the GNU General Public License v3.0. You are welcome
to read the code that holds your two-factor secrets, build it yourself, or
check any claim on this page:

https://github.com/yassine-idlhaj/Sinope


AVAILABLE IN

English, العربية, Français, Español, Deutsch, Português, Türkçe and 日本語.


PERMISSIONS

Sinope asks for two, and both are optional:

• Camera — only to scan setup QR codes. Frames are decoded on your device and
  never stored or transmitted. You can add every account manually instead.
• Biometric — only for the optional app lock, which is off by default.

Nothing else is requested.


A NOTE ON RESPONSIBILITY

Because Sinope stores nothing outside your phone, nobody can recover your
accounts for you. If you lose the device without a backup, the accounts are
gone. Export an encrypted backup and keep it somewhere safe.

That is the trade for having no one else hold your secrets.
```
